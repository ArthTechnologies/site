# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 25, 2026

## Overview

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

## Data Collection

We collect:
- **Account information:** Email, username, and authentication credentials
- **API credentials:** Instagram and TikTok tokens necessary to operate the service
- **Activity logs:** Which reels were posted, timestamps, and outcomes
- **Usage analytics:** Feature interactions to improve the service

## Data Use

Your data is used to:
- Authenticate and manage your account
- Execute reposting operations across platforms
- Troubleshoot issues and improve service reliability
- Monitor system health and prevent abuse

## Data Storage & Retention

- Account data is retained while your account is active
- Logs are retained for 90 days
- Deleted accounts have associated data removed within 30 days
- API credentials are encrypted at rest

## Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. We integrate with Instagram and TikTok only to perform the services you authorize.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Access your data on request
- Modify or delete your account anytime
- Revoke API permissions through platform settings

## Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure practices. However, no system is completely secure.
